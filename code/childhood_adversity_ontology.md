Childhood adversity ontology
│
├── Childhood exposure (<18 years)
│
├── Childhood trauma type [same segment]
│   ├── Childhood sexual abuse
│   ├── Childhood physical abuse
│   ├── Childhood emotional abuse
│   ├── Childhood neglect
│   ├── Childhood bullying or harassment
│   ├── Childhood family violence
│   ├── Childhood bereavement or loss
│   ├── Childhood institutional adversity
│   └── Childhood interpersonal trauma
│
├── Childhood relational context [same segment]
│   ├── Father mentioned in childhood segment
│   ├── Mother mentioned in childhood segment
│   ├── Sibling mentioned in childhood segment
│   ├── Extended family mentioned in childhood segment
│   └── Peer or school mentioned in childhood segment
│
├── Childhood parental adversity [same segment]
│   ├── Direct paternal abuse
│   ├── Direct maternal abuse
│   ├── Direct parental abuse, unspecified
│   ├── Witnessed parental violence
│   ├── Parental illness or incapacity
│   ├── Parental absence or abandonment
│   └── Family instability
│
├── Childhood sexual-abuse context [same segment]
│   ├── Father mentioned
│   ├── Mother mentioned
│   ├── Sibling mentioned
│   ├── Extended family mentioned
│   ├── Peer or other person mentioned
│   └── Relationship unspecified
│
└── Participant-level childhood profiles
    ├── Number of childhood adversity types
    ├── Repeated or chronic childhood adversity
    ├── Multiple childhood trauma types
    ├── Childhood family adversity
    └── Complex childhood interpersonal adversity
